Features that make SSH obsolete

Termote is packed with everything you need for powerful remote terminal access — without the SSH headache.

Anywhere, Any Network

Ditch VPNs and port forwarding. Termote securely punches through NATs and firewalls, giving you instant access to your machine whether you are on the same Wi-Fi or halfway across the world.

Multi-Pane Terminal

Don't limit yourself to one screen. Split, stack, and manage multiple terminal panes simultaneously right in your browser. Like tmux, but visual.

Smart Single-Instance

Already have Termote running? Typing 'termote' in a new local folder or clicking 'Open with Termote' won't spawn a redundant server. It intelligently connects to your active session.

Zero-Install Browser GUI

Forget downloading bulky SSH clients on your phone or tablet. Any device with a browser becomes your command center with a beautiful, responsive UI.

Runs on Your PC

You get the full unchained power of your host machine's CLI. Whatever your host PC can do, you can do remotely with near-zero latency. No cloud, no lag.

End-to-End Encrypted

Your terminal sessions are wrapped in encrypted WebSockets over HTTPS. Authentication via Microsoft Dev Tunnels ensures only you can access your machine.

Bypasses Firewalls

Runs over Port 443 (HTTPS/WSS). Slices through aggressive corporate and school networks undetected. No special network configuration needed.

No Port Forwarding

Unlike SSH, you don't need to configure your router or firewall. Termote handles the connection through Microsoft's infrastructure.
